# Platform Considerations

We have three platforms, web, iOS, and android. We prioritize the use of our design system and component libraries where possible, and look for accessible options when 3rd party solutions are needed.

## General

Our design system and component libraries are accessible by default and we use platform-native solutions whenever possible. We look for accessible options when 3rd party solutions are needed.

User experience is our first priority and we strive to ensure all patterns follow accessibility best practices.

## Web

Our website is built using React, and we prioritize using our own components whenever possible, including our own CSS library. Avoid 3rd party solutions when possible, and when 3rd party solutions are needed, look for accessible options.

## iOS

Our iOS app is built on SwiftUI and we prioritize using native solutions whenever possible. Should a custom component be required, accessibility is a priority in its implementation.

## Android

Our Android app is built on Kotlin using native solutions whenever possible. Should a custom component be required, accessibility is a priority in its implementation.
