# Accessibility Policies

Our products strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 AA and to EN 301 549 v.3.2.1. When issues are discovered in our products, we implement deadlines to fix them depending on their priority and user impact:

- P1: as soon as possible (<3 working days)
- P2: 10 working days
- P3: 30 working days
- P4: 60 working days

## WCAG 2.2 AA

Our products are tested against the WCAG 2.2 A and AA guidelines. This includes our website and mobile applications. We do not test for AAA guidelines at this time.

## EN 301 549 v.3.2.1

Our products are tested against the EN 301 549 v.3.2.1 guidelines. This includes our website and mobile applications. Our website is tested against the requirements of Chapter 9. Our mobile apps are tested against the requirements of Chapter 11.
